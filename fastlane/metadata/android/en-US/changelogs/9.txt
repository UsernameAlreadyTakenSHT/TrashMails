Security review, five angles:
- A crafted email can no longer freeze the app or crash it at launch
- Oversized links and subjects no longer crash the app
- Kept emails go after a week; no snapshot in recent apps (Android 13+)
- Generic user agent and nothing left on disk by the HTML view
- Sensitive copies flagged on Android 10-12; public addresses copied as sensitive
- DropMail.me token kept on firewall errors
- Every build dependency checked against its SHA-256
